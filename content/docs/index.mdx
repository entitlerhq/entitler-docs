Entitler manages your plans, feature access, usage limits and customer grants. You define features and plans in the dashboard, and your servers and apps ask the Entitler API what each customer is entitled to. When you change a plan, the answers change with it, with no deploy.

## How it fits together

- **Features** are the things you sell: a switch such as `priority_support`, a metered allowance such as `ai_credits`, or a configuration value such as a seat count.
- **Plans** give features to customers. A customer's entitlements come from their plan, their add-ons and any grants you give them.
- **Customers** are your users or accounts, named by your own ID, such as `ada`.
- **Usage** draws down metered features. Entitler counts it, refuses it past the allowance when you ask it to, and resets it on the schedule the feature sets.
