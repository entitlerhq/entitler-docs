---
title: Change a customer's plan
description: Move a customer to another plan from your server, or let them choose one themselves with checkout.
---

There are two ways to change a customer's plan: you move them from your server, or they choose a plan in your app. Both name the plan by its key, and both answer the customer's holdings after the move.

## Move a customer from your server

Use `PUT /v1/customers/{external_id}/plan` for moves you decide: a deal your sales team closed, a migration, a support case. Your key needs `customers:write`.

```bash
curl -X PUT "$ENTITLER_API/v1/customers/ada/plan" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ENTITLER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"set": {"planKey": "pro"}, "reason": "Annual deal", "register": true}'
```

| Field | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| `set` | The plan, by `planKey`. |
| `periodId` | The billing period, such as monthly or yearly, by its ID from your project settings. |
| `when` | `SUBSCRIPTION_MOVE_WHEN_NOW` or `SUBSCRIPTION_MOVE_WHEN_PERIOD_END`. Your project's move policy decides when you leave it out. |
| `reason` | Why, for the customer's history in the dashboard. |
| `register` | `true` registers the customer first when they don't exist yet. |
| `expectedRevision` | The customer's revision you last read, so the move is refused with `REVISION_CONFLICT` if someone changed them since. |

A move booked for the end of the period shows in the answer as pending. Cancel it with `POST /v1/customers/{external_id}/subscription:cancelPendingChange`.

## Let the customer choose

Use `POST /v1/customers/{external_id}/subscription` for moves the customer makes in your app. It runs your move policies and, when you have connected a payment provider, takes payment.

```bash
curl -X POST "$ENTITLER_API/v1/customers/ada/subscription" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ENTITLER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"plan": {"planKey": "pro"}, "returnUrl": "https://app.example.com/billing"}'
```

The answer is one of three outcomes:

- `done`: the move is made, and its holdings are the customer's new plans.
- `pay`: a payment is due first. Send the customer to `pay.url`, the checkout page; they come back to your `returnUrl`, and the move completes once they pay.
- `confirming`: the payment provider is confirming a payment. The move completes on its own when it does.

Your app can make this call itself with a [customer token](/keys-and-tokens#customer-tokens) that holds `billing:self`, naming the customer as `me`.

## Show the plans a customer can choose

`GET /v1/customers/{external_id}/plans` lists the plans the customer can move to: whether each is an upgrade or a downgrade, the features it changes, its billing periods and when the move would happen. Use it to show the right buttons on your pricing page.

## Cancel

`POST /v1/customers/{external_id}/subscription:cancel` cancels the customer's subscription, at the time your move policy sets. They keep their plan until then, then fall back to your default plan.
