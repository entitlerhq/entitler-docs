---
title: Give a customer a grant
description: Give one customer a feature or extra allowance outside their plan, for a number of days or for good.
---

A grant gives one customer a feature value their plan doesn't: priority support for a trial, extra AI credits to make up for an outage, a higher limit for a deal. It lasts the days you choose, or until you revoke it, and needs no change to their plan.

## Give a grant

Turn a switch on for 14 days:

```bash
curl -X POST "$ENTITLER_API/v1/customers/ada/grants" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ENTITLER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"featureKey": "priority_support", "value": {"on": {}}, "days": 14, "reason": "Trial of priority support"}'
```

Add 500 to a metered allowance, with no end:

```bash
curl -X POST "$ENTITLER_API/v1/customers/ada/grants" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ENTITLER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"featureKey": "ai_credits", "value": {"limit": {"count": 500}}, "reason": "Credit for the outage on 3 October"}'
```

| Field | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| `featureKey` | The feature to grant. A group can't be granted; grant its features. |
| `value` | `{"on": {}}` for a switch, or `{"limit": {"count": n}}` or `{"limit": {"unlimited": {}}}` for a metered or configuration feature. |
| `days` | How long the grant lasts, up to 3,650. Leave it out for a grant with no end. |
| `reason` | Why you gave it, kept with the grant in the customer's history. |

The answer is the grant, with its `id`, `from` and `until`. Your key needs `customers:write`.

## How grants add up

- A switch grant turns the feature on.
- A metered grant adds to the customer's allowance, on top of their plan and add-ons.
- A configuration grant replaces the plan's value when it is larger.

The customer's entitlement lists each grant in `sources`, and `nextChangeAt` says when the next one ends.

## Revoke a grant

```bash
curl -X POST "$ENTITLER_API/v1/customers/ada/grants/$GRANT_ID:revoke" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ENTITLER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"reason": "Trial ended early"}'
```

A customer can hold up to 50 grants that are live now or start later.
